About us.

City and Community Response for Good Governance is a civic initiative promoting good governance, citizen participation and community empowerment across Nigeria.

Who we are

Good governance is a shared responsibility.

Leaders must serve with integrity, and citizens must be informed, engaged and ready to take part. We exist to connect the two, through dialogue, awareness and community action.

We work with young people, community leaders, volunteers and civil society groups to build communities that ask for transparency, responsible leadership and results. We stand behind the Renewed Hope Agenda and the re-election of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu in 2027, and we campaign peacefully and lawfully.

Our mission

To promote transparency, accountability and responsible leadership by informing citizens and mobilising communities.

Our vision

An informed, empowered and engaged society, where citizens take part in the decisions that shape their lives.

Our focus

Civic education, advocacy for accountability, community mobilisation, youth engagement and digital awareness.

Our core values.

01

Accountability

Leaders answer for how they use public trust and public money.

02

Transparency

Citizens have a right to see how decisions are made.

03

Citizen participation

Governance works best when people take part in it.

04

Community empowerment

Informed communities can speak and act for themselves.

05

Integrity and responsibility

We hold ourselves to the standards we ask of others.

06

Inclusion and equity

Every voice counts, whatever a person's background.

What we do.

Civic education and awareness

Helping citizens understand their rights, their responsibilities and how government works.

Advocacy for accountability

Speaking up peacefully for transparent, responsible leadership.

Community engagement

Taking the conversation about governance into neighbourhoods and towns.

Public awareness campaigns

Sharing clear, accurate information with the public.

Community mobilisation

Bringing people together around common goals and collective action.

Youth engagement

Giving young people a real voice in the life of the nation.

Why it matters

Governance is felt in everyday life.

It shows up in schools and clinics, roads and safety, jobs and opportunity. When leaders are accountable and citizens are involved, public resources reach the people they are meant for.

That is the change we work for, one community at a time.
