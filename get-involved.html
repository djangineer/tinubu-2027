Participation guidelines

Ground rules for everyone.

  • Be respectful online and offline. No abuse, threats or harassment.
  • Share information only from verified, official sources.
  • Never pay or accept money, gifts or favours for votes.
  • Follow electoral law and INEC guidelines.
  • Respect privacy. Do not collect others' data without consent.
  • Represent yourself as an independent supporter, not as an official of the campaign, unless authorised.

Community and public engagement.

Listening forums, door-to-door conversations and town halls work best when neighbours organise them. Contact us to plan one.
