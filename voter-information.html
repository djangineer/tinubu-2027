Voter information.

Plain steps to register, collect your PVC and vote. Always confirm details with INEC.

Five steps to vote.

  1. Check you are registered

    Visit the INEC portal to confirm your details.

  2. Register or update

    Register as a new voter, or request a transfer or replacement through INEC.

  3. Collect your PVC

    Collect it in person as INEC directs. Never sell or lend it.

  4. Find your polling unit

    Confirm where you will vote.

  5. Vote on election day

    Follow INEC's accreditation and voting procedure at your unit.
